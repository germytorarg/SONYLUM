## Rules

- You own ONLY the scene files named in your task. Overwrite the stub files there (they were generated by stubgen). Never edit any other file (no lib.js, core.js, timeline.js, music.js, docs, tools). If you need a helper lib lacks, define it inside your scene file.
- Each file registers exactly one shot: `FILM.scene({ id: '<id>', draw(ctx, t, info) {...} })`, inside an IIFE with `'use strict'`.
- Deterministic and stateless: no Math.random/Date/performance.now/crypto; seed everything from `LIB.hash(ID, ...)`; draw from `t` alone; caches only for t-independent geometry/sprites (`L.cached(key, fn)`); clamp `t` to `[0, info.dur]` first.
- Frame 0 of every shot is a fully composed frame; the last frame holds.
- No text at all (the only text in the film is the wordmark in 21, owned by its agent).
- Must-read content inside x 60–940, y 220–1540. Scenery runs full bleed.
- Illustrated shots: `lib.stripes` background (band A stripeCream, band B per act from the storyboard), ink outlines via inkPath with boil wobble, hatching on shadow sides (light from upper left), at least one overlay (annMagenta / annBlue / annYellow rings, dashed trajectories, rulers, arcs). Characters/objects move on twos (`L.onTwos`), camera/overlays at 24 fps.
- Schematic shots: start from `lib.blueprint`, double lavender outlines, lattices, glow dots, brackets/ticks, network nodes, magenta only for moments of change (≤ 12 frames), the progress glyph helper copied verbatim from the storyboard.
- Density is part of the look: aim for the finish of the butterfly example (hundreds of hatch strokes, rich detail). Thin, timid frames are the default failure. Large subjects fill 60–90 percent of the frame width.
- Respect match-cut geometry exactly (G1 bean, G2 cherry, G3 cup, G4 spouts): draw them screen-fixed (not under a camera transform) on the frames where the cut happens, or compensate the camera.
- Keep frame cost reasonable (target under ~80 ms per frame; cache static layers with `L.cached` keyed on everything they depend on, including the boil index if they wobble).

## Workflow per shot

1. Write `src/scenes/NN-<id>.js`.
2. `node /home/user/SONYLUM/films/del-cafeto-a-la-taza/tools/snap.cjs --shot <id> --only --samples 6 --sheet` and LOOK at the contact sheet (Read the PNG under `.frames/`). Also snap single full-size frames at key beats with `--shot <id> --only --times <t1>,<t2>` (times shot-local) and look at them.
3. Fix and re-snap until the sheet is on-brief: correct subject facts, dense finished drawing, beats landing on their frames, match-cut geometry on its pixels.
4. Before finishing, verify your files throw no errors at the first, middle and last frame and around transitions, and that two snaps of the same frame in different orders are identical (e.g. snap `--times 0,1,2` and `--times 2,1,0` and compare file hashes with `sha1sum`).
5. Do not run `tools/check.cjs` or `tools/render.cjs` (the director runs the gate), and do not commit.
